Ornament Engine · Experiment 1 and sandbox

Does the score see plants?

Experiment 1 tests, blind, whether choosing parents by score version 1 or version 2 gives more plant-like images than choosing at random. The sandbox is for exploring: your star dampener, equal-area zones, and the mirror switch.

1 · Generate

The tool randomly assigns 69 runs to three groups of 23, runs each for 60 generations, and keeps each run's final image. You won't see any images until rating starts, and never which group an image came from.

Results

Pre-registered tests

Two-sided tests. Pairwise p-values are Holm-corrected for three comparisons. Rank-biserial r runs from −1 to 1; positive means the first group was rated higher.

Exploratory: which measures track your ratings (all 69 images)

Spearman correlations with approximate p-values. Exploratory, not pre-registered.
